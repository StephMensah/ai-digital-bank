04

Your core stays the book of record

Built to the Mambu v2 contract

Version-negotiated requests, UUID idempotency keys, and an in-flight retry treated as in flight — never as a second payment.

Postings through an outbox

Settlement writes the ledger first and queues the core posting, so a slow core never loses or doubles money.

Reconciled every day

Account balances are checked against the ledger; any break surfaces in the control tower, not in a customer complaint.
