FIDELITY AI-FIRST BANK — prototype
Strategic AI Banking Framework, built out as running software.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
QUICKEST LOOK  (no install, no internet needed)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Double-click  index.html
  It is the prototype hub: pick a surface, switch between phone,
  tablet and laptop widths, and the preview resizes live.

  Or open any product on its own:
    fidelity-app.html        the mobile bank
    fidelity-web.html        internet banking, personal + business
    reviewer-console.html    staff: the escalation queue
    control-tower.html       staff: portfolio and governance

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EVERYTHING CONNECTED  (needs Python 3.10+, nothing else)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  python3 decision_api.py
  → http://localhost:8765/

  Now the four surfaces share one queue. Escalate something in the
  app and it reaches the reviewer's console within four seconds.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WORTH TRYING IN THE APP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  · Send GHS 200 to Kojo Mensah — the balance moves, the statement
    grows, the running balance recalculates.
  · Pay Global Marine Ltd — sanctions hold, money never leaves.
  · Send GHS 15,000 — stopped by your Tier 2 limit before any model
    runs.
  · Save › Rent — Dansoman › MTN MoMo — fund a goal from mobile
    money instead of your account.
  · Push the loan slider past your limit — no model declines you; a
    named credit officer decides.
  · Ask about a loan in Ga or Hausa — below the accuracy floor it
    hands to a person and says why.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE ANY EXTERNAL DEMO
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Every model is a stub. Agent.infer() declares the endpoint it
     should call; replace its body and that agent is live. Governance,
     escalation, audit and value tracking need no changes.
  2. The Twi strings were written by me, not a speaker. Have branch
     staff in Kumasi or Tamale review them first.

  Merchant logos are generated brand tiles, not real assets — see
  logos/README.md for how to drop licensed ones in.
